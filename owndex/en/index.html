Owndex: Warranty Tracker

Receipt Keeper & Return Alerts

Track warranties and return windows, keep receipt photos, get timely reminders.

Download on the App Store

Available on iPhone. The Android version is in testing.

What it does

  • Keep proof of purchase: attach receipt and product photos from your camera or photo library
  • Warranty reminders: choose to be notified 30, 15, 7, or 1 day before a warranty expires
  • Return reminders: get a heads-up before the return window closes
  • Organize by category: electronics, appliances, furniture, vehicles, tools, clothing, jewelry, and more
  • Search and filter by active, expiring soon, or expired
  • Private by design: your data stays on your device and no account is needed
  • Unlimited items

Frequently asked questions

How can I keep track of all my product warranties?

The simplest way is to log each purchase the day you buy it. In Owndex you take a photo of the receipt and the product, enter the purchase date and warranty length, and the app calculates the end date. It then reminds you 30, 15, 7, or 1 day before the warranty expires, depending on what you choose.

Is a photo of my receipt enough as proof of purchase?

Many retailers and manufacturers accept a clear copy of the receipt, but policies vary, so check the warranty terms and keep the original when it is required. Storing a sharp photo protects you if the paper fades or gets lost. Owndex keeps the receipt photo next to the product, and Owndex Pro can export a PDF to attach to a warranty claim.

How do I remember return deadlines?

Enter the return period when you add an item and let a reminder do the remembering. Owndex tracks each item's return window separately from its warranty and warns you before the window closes, so you can decide whether to keep or return something while you still have the option.

Do I need an account to use a warranty tracker app?

Not with Owndex. Everything is stored on your device and no sign-up is required. If you want a safety copy, Owndex Pro lets you back up and restore with your own cloud storage: a backup file in Files or iCloud Drive on iPhone, or your own Google Drive or OneDrive folder on Android.

Is Owndex free?

Owndex has a free version that tracks up to 10 items and shows ads. Owndex Pro removes ads and adds unlimited items, PDF export for warranty claims, and backup and restore. Pro is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does Owndex read receipts automatically?

No. Owndex stores photos of your receipts and products exactly as you take them, and you enter the key details such as purchase date, warranty length, and return period yourself. This keeps the app simple, private, and reliable, and the reminders are based on the dates you set.
