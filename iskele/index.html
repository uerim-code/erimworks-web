Iskele: Boat Color Sort Puzzle

Crowd & Seat Sorting Games

Sort the crowd onto Istanbul's ferries by color. Rope-tied pairs, hidden riders and fish crates turn every trip into a calm brain puzzle with no timer.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Iskele is a calm boat color sort puzzle set on the Istanbul ferry piers. The quay is crowded with passengers, every boat takes one color, and you decide who boards first. Tap a passenger with a clear path, send them onto the matching ferry and clear the jam before the waiting bench fills up.

How to play

  • Tap any passenger who has a free path to the walkway.
  • Passengers in the docked boat's color board at once; the rest wait on the bench.
  • The bench has five seats. Plan the order of the crowd, or it fills up.

More than a simple sorting game

  • Tied pairs: two passengers joined by a rope step out together.
  • Hidden passengers show their color only when they can reach the walkway.
  • Fish crates block the way and never move.
  • Early-departure tickets send the docked ferry round again without losing a seat.

A real Istanbul ferry route

Sail pier to pier along the Bosphorus: Eminönü, Karaköy, Kadıköy, Üsküdar, Ortaköy, Bebek and on to the Princes' Islands, 30 piers in all. Every level is generated for you and proven solvable before you play it. Clear it in few moves to earn up to three stars.

A relaxing brain puzzle for short breaks

  • No timer and no lives: play at your own pace.
  • Offline game: plays without Wi-Fi or internet.
  • Painted boat-enamel art, harbor sounds and gentle vibration.
  • Available in 12 languages.

If you like color sort puzzles, seat sorting and crowd puzzle games, or traffic puzzles where the order of every move matters, Iskele gives you that satisfying moment when everything falls into place, out on the water.

Iskele is free to play with ads. A one-time purchase removes the ads between trips; rewarded videos always stay optional.
