UNVPortal: laboratory, examination and quality management on one platform

UNVPortal is a browser-based modular management platform for universities and higher education institutions. It runs laboratory operations, examination processes, and quality and accreditation work from shared unit, staff and course definitions. Three modules are in production: Labs (laboratory management), Exams (examination management) and Quality (quality management); Connect and Intelligence are in preparation. Unit, staff, course and permission definitions are entered once, and all three modules read the same record.

What is a university management platform?

A university management platform is modular software that runs a higher education institution's academic operations, such as laboratories, examinations, quality and accreditation, from shared definitions. The unit tree, staff, course and permission definitions are entered once; every module reads the same record, and the same information is not entered twice. UNVPortal is a university management platform of this kind, working through its Labs, Exams and Quality modules.

What a single record means in practice

In universities, laboratory, examination and quality work usually runs in three separate applications or three separate spreadsheets. The same lecturer is defined in three places, the same course code is written three different ways, and evidence for the quality report is collected piece by piece.

In UNVPortal, the unit tree, staff records, course and programme definitions and permissions are held in one place: Labs and Exams read from the same database. Quality runs on its own database and only reads those records — when it asks for a piece of evidence it links directly to the instrument usage report kept in the laboratory or to the programme outcome result calculated in the Exams module, without writing anything back to the running system. The source of every piece of evidence is recorded, and only the fields actually needed are carried over. When reporting time comes, there is nothing left to collect.

The separation is deliberate: quality work carries no risk of breaking a laboratory or examination system already in use. Remove Quality and Labs and Exams keep running exactly as before.

Frequently asked questions

Can we license the modules separately?

UNVPortal modules can be licensed separately: Labs, Exams and Quality each work on their own. When used together, unit, staff and permission definitions are entered once: Labs and Exams read from the same database, and Quality only reads those records. The same information is not entered twice.

Where is the data held?

UNVPortal data is held either in a cloud deployment managed by Erimworks or on the institution's own server, as the institution prefers. Each institution's data is isolated as a separate tenant record.

Does it talk to our existing student information system?

UNVPortal's connections to identity providers, student information systems, document management and HR systems will be set up through the UNVPortal Connect module. Because Connect is still in preparation, connections in current deployments are configured one by one. Tell us which systems you use and we can define the scope.

How does pricing work?

UNVPortal pricing follows an enterprise licence model; it is quoted based on the number of users and the selected modules. For a quote and a live demo, write to contact@erimworks.com.

Is a trial deployment possible?

A trial deployment of UNVPortal is possible. After the demo, a deployment with sample data is opened for your institution and access is set up for authorised users.
