Your eyes need a break every 20 minutes. Restla makes sure you take it.

Restla is a 20-20-20 eye break timer for iPhone. Press Start once; every 20 minutes it taps you, you look at something far away for 20 seconds, you tap “I looked away”, and the timer runs on. No account, works offline, free. Pro removes the ad banner for a one-time $1.99.

Download on the App Store How it works

A timer that asks, so the break actually happens

It asks, you confirm. When the 20 seconds are up, the break screen waits for one tap: “I looked away”. Only confirmed breaks count toward your day and your streak. Skipped ones are not held against you, they just do not count.

It works when you forget. The reminder comes as a notification, the countdown shows on a home screen widget, and the timer keeps its place when the app is in the background. Close the tab, lock the phone, come back; it is still running.

There is nothing to manage. No tasks, no goals, no account. Two modes: the 20-20-20 rule, or a Pomodoro of 25 minutes focus with a 5 minute rest every four rounds. Pause for a meeting when you need silence for 30, 60 or 90 minutes.

How it works

  1. Set your work interval. The default is 20 minutes of work and a 20 second break; anything from 5 to 90 minutes is one tap away in Settings.
  2. Get a nudge. A notification and a dark break screen with a single red hand sweeping 20 seconds. Look at something about 6 metres (20 feet) away.
  3. Tap “I looked away”. Your breaks today and your day streak go up. Seven days of timer minutes and breaks are kept on the phone.

Questions

Is the 20-20-20 rule proven?

The American Optometric Association and the American Academy of Ophthalmology both recommend it. The research is still thin: the best study so far (29 computer users, 2023) found eye-strain and dry-eye symptoms dropped while people got reminders and came back a week after the reminders stopped. So it seems to help while you do it, and nobody has shown it prevents anything. Restla is a reminder, not a treatment.

Does Restla work offline?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no server. Your settings and stats stay on your phone. The free version shows an ad banner, which needs a connection to load; nothing else does.

What does Pro cost and what does it do?

Pro is a one-time $1.99 purchase that removes the ad banner. That is all it does: modes, stats, the widget and every setting are free for everyone. There is no subscription.

Will it interrupt me on a call?

Pause for a meeting silences breaks for 30, 60 or 90 minutes and then picks up where it left off. When a break is due, you get a notification; you choose when to open it, and Skip is always one tap.

Is there an Android version?

Restla for Android is in testing on Google Play and will be released after the iPhone update. Write to contact@erimworks.com if you want to hear when it ships.
