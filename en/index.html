Management systems for universities and clinics

Five management platforms for laboratory, examination, quality, patient-operations and course-administration processes. Erimworks is an independent software studio based in Türkiye, with 8 mobile apps on the App Store and Google Play.

Every deployment is configured around the institution's own business rules and permission scheme. Institutional data is held in its own tenant record, and isolation is enforced in the database layer rather than in the application.

Software and platforms

Platforms are licensed and deployed independently. Within UNVPortal, Labs and Exams read from the same database; Quality runs on its own database and collects evidence by reading those records.

UNVPortal

Three modules in use

A modular management platform for laboratory operations, examination processes, and quality and accreditation work in higher education. The unit tree, staff records, course definitions and permissions are defined once, and all three modules read the same record.

International Patient OS

In use

An operations system for clinics treating patients from abroad, in which the entire process from enquiry to discharge and the closing of an incentive file is run on a single case record.

  • Unified inbox
  • Surgeon review
  • Travel and surgery planning
  • Per-case finance
  • Incentive files

KursOS

In use

Covers lesson scheduling, student and instructor management, collections and instructor earnings for courses, art schools and studios. Deployment is carried out under the institution's own brand and domain.

  • Recurring lesson series
  • Rooms and capacity
  • Debt tracking
  • Three pay models
  • White-label deployment
Details

Two further UNVPortal modules are in preparation: Connect, for integration with the institution's identity provider, student information and document management systems, and Intelligence, for document and regulation analysis.
