Contact

All enquiries are handled through contact@erimworks.com. Institutional enquiries are answered within a business day, app support requests within two business days.

Write to us

Types of request

The links below pre-fill the subject line. If you prefer to write directly, the address is the same.

Live demo request

A screen-sharing session of about forty minutes is arranged. Once you send your institution's name, the expected number of users and the modules you are interested in, the first reply proposes times.

Request a demo

Pricing quote and enterprise licensing

The licence is based on the number of users and the selected modules. If you state your institution's purchasing method (direct procurement, tender, framework agreement), the quote is prepared in that form.

Request a quote

Trial deployment

After the demo, a deployment with sample data is opened for your institution and access is set up for authorised users. Data can be entered during the trial; if the decision is positive, the same deployment goes live.

Request a trial

App support

Questions about the apps published on the App Store and Google Play, purchase and subscription problems, and account deletion requests go to the same address. Stating the app and device you use helps resolve the request faster.

Request support

Press, partnerships and other

Joint projects, integrations and press enquiries are also handled through the same address.
