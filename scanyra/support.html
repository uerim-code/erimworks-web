Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a document?

Tap the scan button, point your camera at the document, and Scanyra detects the edges automatically. You can capture multiple pages and export them as a single PDF.

Is text recognition (OCR) free?

Yes. OCR runs on your device and is always free: extract, search and copy text with no internet connection required.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside the app. On iPhone/iPad: open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions, select Scanyra, and change or cancel. On Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you already subscribed (e.g., on another device or after reinstalling), open the Premium screen and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to purchase.

The subscription prices won't load. What can I do?

Prices come from the App Store / Google Play. Check your internet connection, close and reopen the app, and make sure you're signed in to the store. If it still doesn't load, email us and we'll help.

Where are my documents stored?

On your device. Your scans and recognized text never leave your phone unless you choose to share or export them.
